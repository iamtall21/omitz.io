DOE Voucher Program · Voucher Opportunity 3

Code training for the jurisdictions that permit new energy projects.

Omitz, LLC is a selected provider in the U.S. Department of Energy Voucher Program, administered by EnergyWerx. Through Voucher Opportunity 3, authorities having jurisdiction and other entities preparing to host advanced energy projects can apply for in-kind support, including NFPA 2 and NFPA 855 training for code officials and first responders, at no cost to the recipient.

Recipient applications due December 16, 2026 at 3:00 PM ET

Why it fits

Training your staff before the first application arrives

Educate first responders

Voucher Opportunity 3 names first responder education as eligible support. Omitz courses cover the hazards, detection, suppression and emergency response provisions firefighters need before an incident, not after.

Review plans with confidence

Plan reviewers, fire marshals and building officials learn what NFPA 2 and NFPA 855 require, what a complete submittal looks like and which questions to ask a developer.

Taught by a practitioner

Phil Clark, MS, CIH, CSP, has worked on hydrogen and energy storage safety at a national laboratory since 2013 and is a lead NFPA 2 instructor for the AIChE Center for Hydrogen Safety.

  • NFPA 2: Hydrogen Technologies Code
  • NFPA 855: Stationary Energy Storage Systems

How to apply

Requesting Omitz in your application

  1. Confirm eligibility. Read the Voucher Opportunity 3 page for eligible entities and technology areas.
  2. Find Omitz in the Capabilities Tool. Open the DOE pre-approved provider list, filter by organization and select “Omitz, LLC”.
  3. List Omitz as your preference. In the recipient application, choose the Omitz capability. Disclose any previous or current work with Omitz, as the program requires.
  4. Submit by the deadline. Applications close December 16, 2026 at 3:00 PM ET. DOE reviews applications and makes all selections and matches.
