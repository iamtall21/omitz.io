DOE Voucher Opportunity 3 · Apply by Dec 16, 2026, 3:00 PM ET

Request hydrogen and battery storage safety training for your department

The DOE Voucher Program can provide live, in-person safety training from Omitz at no cost to your department. It takes one application on the EnergyWerx form, about 20 minutes.

  1. Contact and department info. Name, address with ZIP+4, and your UEI if you have one.
  2. Technology area. Pick one: Hydrogen or Long-duration energy storage (BESS). You don’t need both.
  3. Capability Choice 1: VO3-3. VO3-3 is Omitz. Pick it first.
  4. Five short questions. Use our example answers as a starting point.
  5. Agree and submit by December 16 at 3:00 PM ET.
Open the application (EnergyWerx form) See example answers

Step by step

Filling in the form, with example answers

Treat every example as a template. They’re written for a typical fire department to show the kind of detail each question needs. Rewrite as much as you need to, all of it if necessary, so each answer describes your jurisdiction, your projects, your people and your dates. The [bracketed] parts are the minimum to replace. DOE reads these answers to decide on and match requests, so your own specifics matter more than our wording.

  1. Contact and department info

    • Enter a main point of contact (the person who would schedule training) and, ideally, an alternate.
    • Use your department’s or jurisdiction’s official name and address, the same way on every submission.
    • ZIP+4 asks for the last 4 digits only. Find them with the USPS ZIP Code lookup.
    • UEI: answer Yes, In Process, or No. You can apply without one. Registration is free at SAM.gov.

  2. Technology area

    Pick one: Long-duration energy storage for battery storage (BESS) sites, or Hydrogen for hydrogen production, storage or fueling. You don’t need both. A department with only a BESS project, or only a hydrogen project, qualifies the same way. VO3-3 covers either one.

  3. Capability Choice 1: VO3-3 (Omitz)

    Open the Capability Choice 1 dropdown and select VO3-3 - Hydrogen and Energy Storage Systems Safety Trainings (Live, In-Person). That’s the Omitz training. You can leave Choices 2 to 5 blank. Omitz, LLC is also listed in the DOE Capabilities Tool.

  4. Five short questions

    Each answer can be up to 250 words. Questions marked ** are shared with providers, so leave out anything confidential. Copy an example only as a starting point, then rewrite it in your own words.

    1. Prior relationship with the voucher provider

    Form asks: “Describe any prior relationship(s) you have with the voucher provider(s) that you would like to receive support from.”

    N/A. We have no prior relationship with Omitz, LLC. We learned about its VO3-3 training through [the Omitz website / LinkedIn / a colleague]. [If you have taken an Omitz course or worked with Omitz before, describe that here instead.]

    2. Your technology

    Form asks: “Please describe your technology briefly, including typical system size (e.g., MW and duration) and use cases.”

    [Town/County] has [a BESS project / a hydrogen project / both] in planning that our department will review, inspect and protect. [Keep only the bullet that applies.] - A [100 MW / 400 MWh] lithium-ion battery energy storage system (BESS) proposed at [location], to [store solar generation and support the local grid]. - A [hydrogen fueling station / 5 MW electrolyzer] at [location], serving [transit buses / an industrial user]. Our role is plan review, inspection and emergency response for these sites. We expect more storage and hydrogen proposals as [the utility’s storage program / state incentives] grow.

    3. Background

    Form asks: “Briefly describe your organization. Briefly describe past or current efforts that are relevant to the voucher support you request.”

    [Department name] provides fire protection, EMS and fire code enforcement for [population] residents across [area] in [county, state]. We run [number] stations with [number] career and [number] volunteer personnel. Our fire marshal’s office reviews plans and inspects commercial sites under the [2024] International Fire Code, which points to NFPA 855 for energy storage and NFPA 2 for hydrogen. So far we have [met with the developer / reviewed preliminary site plans / toured a similar site], but our staff have no formal training on battery storage or hydrogen hazards.

    4. Justification of need

    Form asks: “Describe your organization’s needs as they relate to the voucher opportunity and capability(ies) you prefer. Include justification for the timing of the voucher support and why you are ready to receive support.”

    The [BESS project] is scheduled for [permit review in March 2027 and commissioning in late 2027]. Before then, our plan reviewers need to know what NFPA 855 and NFPA 2 require, and our crews need to understand thermal runaway, deflagration, hydrogen leaks and safe response tactics. We would train about [40] people: [inspectors, company officers and line firefighters, plus mutual aid partners from neighboring departments]. [We have no budget for specialized training this year.] Live, in-person training lets crews work through scenarios based on the sites in our area. We are ready to schedule training as soon as a match is made.

    5. Catalytic effects

    Form asks: “Describe your near-term plans and how the voucher support you request will help you achieve them at an accelerated pace.”

    With trained staff, we can [review the BESS permit application on our normal timeline] instead of hiring outside reviewers, write pre-incident plans with the operators before the sites go live, and respond safely if something goes wrong. Training now also prepares us for the [number] additional storage and hydrogen proposals we expect over the next [three] years, so future projects in [jurisdiction] can move through permitting faster.

  5. Agree and submit

    • I am representing: fire departments and building or permitting offices usually pick Government - Authority Having Jurisdiction (State, Local, Tribal).
    • DOE funding, past or pending: answer Yes or No, and explain briefly if either is Yes.
    • How did you hear about EnergyWerx: check what’s true.
    • Check I agree on all three agreement questions.

    Note to Organizer (optional)

    Form asks: “Note to Organizer”

    We are requesting VO3-3, Hydrogen and Energy Storage Systems Safety Trainings (Live, In-Person), with Omitz, LLC as our provider.

    Press Submit before December 16, 2026 at 3:00 PM ET. After the deadline, DOE reviews requests and proposes matches, and you can accept or decline yours.

Why it fits

Training your staff before the first application arrives

Educate first responders

Voucher Opportunity 3 names first responder education as eligible support. Omitz courses cover the hazards, detection, suppression and emergency response provisions firefighters need before an incident, not after.

Review plans with confidence

Plan reviewers, fire marshals and building officials learn what NFPA 2 and NFPA 855 require, what a complete submittal looks like and which questions to ask a developer.
