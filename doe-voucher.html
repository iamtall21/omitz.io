DOE Voucher Opportunity 3 · Apply by Dec 16, 2026, 3:00 PM ET

Request hydrogen and battery storage safety training for your department

The DOE Voucher Program can provide live, in-person safety training from Omitz at no cost to your department. It takes one application on the EnergyWerx form, about 20 minutes.

  1. Contact and organization info. Name, address with ZIP+4, and your UEI if you have one.
  2. Technology area. Pick one: Hydrogen or Long-duration energy storage (BESS). You don’t need both.
  3. Capability Choice 1: VO3-3. VO3-3 is Omitz. Pick it first.
  4. Five short questions. Use our example answers as a starting point.
  5. Agree and submit by December 16 at 3:00 PM ET.
Open the application (EnergyWerx form) See example answers

Step by step

Filling in the form, with example answers

These are templates, not answers. They work for fire departments, building and permitting offices, and local governments alike. Replace every [bracketed] part with your own details, delete what doesn’t apply, and rewrite any other words so each answer describes your organization, your projects, your people and your dates. DOE reads these answers to decide on and match requests, so your specifics matter more than our wording.

  1. Contact and organization info

    • Enter a main point of contact (the person who would schedule training) and, ideally, an alternate.
    • Use your organizationUse your department’s or jurisdiction’s official namersquo;s official name and address, the same way on every submission.
    • ZIP+4 asks for the last 4 digits only. Find them with the USPS ZIP Code lookup.
    • UEI: answer Yes, In Process, or No. You can apply without one.

  2. Technology area

    Pick one: Long-duration energy storage for battery storage (BESS) sites, or Hydrogen for hydrogen production, storage or fueling. You don’t need both. A department with only a BESS project, or only a hydrogen project, qualifies the same way. VO3-3 covers either one.

  3. Capability Choice 1: VO3-3 (Omitz)

    Open the Capability Choice 1 dropdown and select VO3-3 - Hydrogen and Energy Storage Systems Safety Trainings (Live, In-Person). That’s the Omitz training. You can leave Choices 2 to 5 blank.

  4. Five short questions

    Each answer can be up to 250 words. Questions marked ** are shared with providers, so leave out anything confidential. Copy a template, then replace every bracket with your own details.

    1. Prior relationship with the voucher provider

    Form asks: “Describe any prior relationship(s) you have with the voucher provider(s) that you would like to receive support from.”

    [Describe any prior relationship with Omitz, LLC, such as a past course or project, or write N/A if there is none.]

    2. Your technology

    Form asks: “Please describe your technology briefly, including typical system size (e.g., MW and duration) and use cases.”

    [Organization name] [reviews / permits / inspects / provides emergency response for / plans to host] [a battery energy storage system (BESS) / a hydrogen project / both] in [jurisdiction]. [Keep only the lines below that apply.] - [BESS: system size in MW and MWh or hours of duration, battery type, location, and use, such as storing solar generation or supporting the grid.] - [Hydrogen: system type, such as a fueling station, electrolyzer or storage, its size, location, and use.] Our role is [plan review / permitting / inspection / emergency response / project owner or operator].

    3. Background

    Form asks: “Briefly describe your organization. Briefly describe past or current efforts that are relevant to the voucher support you request.”

    [Organization name] is [a fire department / a building or permitting office / a city, county or tribal government / other] serving [population or area] in [county, state]. [Describe your staff: how many people and their roles, such as plan reviewers, inspectors, firefighters or permit staff.] [Name the codes you enforce or follow, such as your adopted edition of the International Fire Code, NFPA 855 or NFPA 2.] [Describe what you have done on these projects so far, such as meetings with developers, preliminary plan reviews or site visits.]

    4. Justification of need

    Form asks: “Describe your organization’s needs as they relate to the voucher opportunity and capability(ies) you prefer. Include justification for the timing of the voucher support and why you are ready to receive support.”

    [Project name or description] is expected to reach [permit review / construction / commissioning] by [month and year]. Before then, our [staff roles] need training on [the requirements of NFPA 855 and NFPA 2 / battery storage hazards and emergency response / hydrogen hazards and emergency response]. We would train about [number] people, including [roles and any partner agencies]. [Explain why you can’t meet this need now, such as budget or no local training.] [Explain why you are ready now, such as an upcoming review date or staff available for training.]

    5. Catalytic effects

    Form asks: “Describe your near-term plans and how the voucher support you request will help you achieve them at an accelerated pace.”

    With trained staff, we can [list your near-term outcomes, such as completing plan reviews in-house, writing pre-incident plans before a site goes live, or shortening permitting timelines]. [Describe the projects you expect over the next few years and how this training prepares you for them.]

  5. Agree and submit

    • I am representing: fire departments and building or permitting offices usually pick Government - Authority Having Jurisdiction (State, Local, Tribal).
    • DOE funding, past or pending: answer Yes or No, and explain briefly if either is Yes.
    • How did you hear about EnergyWerx: check what’s true.
    • Check I agree on all three agreement questions.

    Note to Organizer (optional)

    Form asks: “Note to Organizer”

    We are requesting VO3-3, Hydrogen and Energy Storage Systems Safety Trainings (Live, In-Person), with Omitz, LLC as our provider.

    Press Submit before December 16, 2026 at 3:00 PM ET. After the deadline, DOE reviews requests and proposes matches, and you can accept or decline yours.

Why it fits

Training your staff before the first application arrives

Educate first responders

Voucher Opportunity 3 names first responder education as eligible support. Omitz courses cover the hazards, detection, suppression and emergency response provisions firefighters need before an incident, not after.

Review plans with confidence

Plan reviewers, fire marshals and building officials learn what NFPA 2 and NFPA 855 require, what a complete submittal looks like and which questions to ask a developer.

Taught by a practitioner

Phil Clark, MS, CIH, CSP, has worked on hydrogen and energy storage safety at a U.S. Department of Energy research facility since 2013 and is a lead NFPA 2 instructor for the AIChE Center for Hydrogen Safety.
