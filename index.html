About

Phil Clark, MS, CIH, CSP

Founder and CEO, Omitz, LLC

Phil is a Certified Industrial Hygienist and Certified Safety Professional with more than 20 years of experience in environmental, health and safety. His career spans military operations, energy research, industrial hygiene, process safety and emerging technologies.

Since 2013 he has worked at the National Laboratory of the Rockies (formerly NREL), specializing in hydrogen safety, energy storage, hazard analysis, gas detection and nanomaterial safety. He is a lead NFPA 2 instructor for the AIChE Center for Hydrogen Safety.
