Career

From the field to the classroom

  1. Military operations · 5 years

    Industrial hygienist, U.S. Navy

    Supported Navy SEAL teams and naval aviation maintenance operations, assessing exposures in some of the most demanding work environments there are.

  2. 2013

    National Laboratory of the Rockies

    Joined the laboratory, then the National Renewable Energy Laboratory, specializing in hydrogen safety, energy storage, hazard analysis, gas detection and nanomaterial safety.

  3. A decade of hydrogen safety

    Hydrogen Safety Point of Contact

    Facilitated hazard analyses and risk assessments, led hazard-awareness training, oversaw construction and operation of research hydrogen systems, and investigated off-normal events.

  4. 2016 edition of NFPA 2

    Code development

    Contributed to the standard permitting checklists developed with the NFPA 2 committee.

  5. Energy storage

    Battery safety in research

    Oversaw destructive abuse testing of battery modules, onboarded large flow battery experiments and guided the addition of battery systems to grid-integration research.

  6. Founder and CEO

    Omitz, LLC

    Founded Omitz to teach the codes that govern hydrogen and energy storage, with courses on NFPA 2 and NFPA 855.

  7. Center for Hydrogen Safety

    Lead NFPA 2 instructor

    Teaches NFPA 2 for the AIChE Center for Hydrogen Safety and serves on its Safety Culture Committee.

  8. 2026

    AI in ESH practice

    Presented a full-day professional development course on ethical AI workflows at AIHA Connect, and contributed to the AIHA publication Using AI to Enhance OEHS.
