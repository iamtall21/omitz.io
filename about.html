About

Phil Clark, MS, CIH, CSP

Founder and CEO, Omitz, LLC

  • Certified Industrial Hygienist (CIH)
  • Certified Safety Professional (CSP)
  • Lead NFPA 2 instructor, AIChE Center for Hydrogen Safety
  • Louisville, Colorado

Phil Clark is a Certified Industrial Hygienist, Certified Safety Professional and entrepreneur with more than 20 years of experience in environmental, health and safety. His career spans military operations, energy research, industrial hygiene, process safety and emerging technologies.

Experience

Phil spent five years as an industrial hygienist supporting U.S. Navy SEAL teams and naval aviation maintenance operations. Since 2013 he has worked at the National Laboratory of the Rockies (formerly the National Renewable Energy Laboratory), where he specializes in hydrogen safety, energy storage systems, hazard analysis, gas detection systems and nanomaterial safety.

As the laboratory’s Hydrogen Safety Point of Contact for a decade, he facilitated hazard analyses and risk assessments, led hazard-awareness training, oversaw the construction and operation of research hydrogen systems, and investigated off-normal events. His energy storage work has included destructive abuse testing of battery modules, onboarding large flow battery experiments and integrating battery systems into grid research.

Omitz

Through Omitz, LLC, Phil provides technical consulting and professional training in hydrogen safety, battery energy storage and the practical application of safety standards, including NFPA 2 and NFPA 855. He serves as a lead NFPA 2 instructor for the AIChE Center for Hydrogen Safety, is a member of the Center’s Safety Culture Committee, and contributed to the standard permitting checklists developed for the 2016 edition of NFPA 2.

AI in ESH practice

Phil is also interested in how artificial intelligence and automation can improve the way organizations work. He helps ESH professionals apply these tools while keeping sound professional judgment, ethical responsibility and appropriate human oversight. He contributed to the AIHA publication Using AI to Enhance OEHS and teaches professional development courses on AI for industrial hygiene and safety practice.

Approach

Phil combines technical expertise and field experience with a commitment to making complex safety and technology concepts understandable and useful. The company’s guiding rule comes from Strunk and White: omit needless words.
