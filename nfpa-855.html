Course outline
Fundamentals (Chapters 1–8)
Administration and definitions; general requirements including construction documents, hazard mitigation analysis, detection and suppression; system interconnections; commissioning; operation and maintenance; decommissioning.
Electrochemical systems (Chapter 9)
UL 9540 listing and UL 9540A fire testing, energy storage management systems, dedicated and non-dedicated locations, size and spacing limits, ventilation, thermal runaway and explosion control, rooftop, garage and mobile installations.
Other technologies (Chapters 11–14)
Fuel cell and flywheel systems, and storage of lithium metal and lithium-ion batteries in warehouses and collection sites.
Residential (Chapter 15)
One- and two-family dwellings and townhouses: permitted locations, energy limits, impact protection and smoke alarms.
Annexes A–H
Battery hazards, firefighting considerations, permits and inspections, and suppression guidance for lithium-ion systems.
